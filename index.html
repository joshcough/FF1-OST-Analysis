NIGHT ROLL — WHAT YOU CAN DO

VIEWS

𝄞 Score / ▦ Roll
Switch between piano roll and engraved sheet music. Same timeline, same everything else. Your choice is remembered.
Drag / pinch
One finger pans; two fingers zoom. In score view, scroll fully left to see the clefs and key signature.

PLAYBACK

▶ Play / ■ Stop
NES-style voices; drum tracks get a real kit. Songs loop at their final bar forever.
⏮
Back to bar 1.
Blue cursor
Play starts here. Tap the bar ruler to place it, or drag its handle (triangle under the ruler). Tapping any note also moves it there.

TRACKS

Track chips (top)
Tap a selected chip to hide that track — sound and visuals, instantly, even mid-playback. Tap again to bring back.
S
Solo: hide everyone else instead.

NOTES & CHORDS

Tap a note
Inspector shows pitch, bar & beat (counted 1e&a), length, velocity, track — and plays it.
⊞ Lasso
Toggle on, then drag a box over notes (works in both views; in the score you can box just one staff). Shows all the pitch names, low to high.
Chord?
After a lasso: reveals what chord those notes make. Hidden by default so you can name it first.

ANNOTATIONS (.rollnotes)

+ Note
Write a note anchored at the cursor's beat. Fill "to bar/beat" to span a range. Ranges glow faint gold in the timeline.
Ruler drag
Drag along the bar ruler to select a range first — + Note pre-fills it.
Gold flags
Existing annotations. Tap one to jump there and read it in the strip above the footer. They also appear as subtitles during playback.
☰ Notes
Every annotation in the song — notes, sections, keys — in one list. Tap a row to edit or delete it; changes to synced notes need a Sync to become permanent.
Sections
Colored bands in the ruler (A, B…). Make one: select a range, + Note, tick "section", give it a short label. Nest them by containment. Tap a band to select its whole range.

KEYS

key? dial
Songs display in C until YOU set the key. Preview any signature live — the right one makes most accidentals vanish.
Set @ bar N
Writes your key choice into the song's notes at the cursor's bar. Set a different key mid-song for key changes — the score draws the new signature there.

EDITING & SYNC

Edit
Reveals Pencil (insert MIDI notes) and Erase. Tucked away so bedtime thumbs don't hit them.
Sync
Commit your annotations, sections, and keys to GitHub (needs your token, one-time), or Copy/Download the file. "Unsynced" = still only on this device.
Load .mid
Open any MIDI file from your device.
