# Quiz Bank

Retrieval-practice questions drawn from what we've actually worked through.
Session starts pull ~5 from here (see supplemental-learning.md for the
concept log each batch comes from). Answers aren't written here on purpose —
they live in the analysis docs, and in Josh.

## Protocol (for Claude)

- Ask ~5 at session start, before new material. One at a time or a short
  batch; let Josh answer before confirming anything.
- Mix recall ("read V/ii aloud") with application ("spell the ii-V-i in
  A minor").
- **Plain language.** No clever metaphors inside the question itself.
- **If Josh says he doesn't understand a question: rephrase it and let him
  answer. Never hand over the answer** — that throws away the retrieval
  moment the quiz exists for. If the answer does get revealed, re-bank the
  concept as `[~]` and re-ask fresh in a later session.
- Log persistent weak spots here as `[~]` and in the song doc's open
  questions.

Status marks: `[ ]` not yet asked · `[x]` answered well · `[~]` shaky, re-ask.
